# Roadmap

Phases are ordered by dependency, not by date. No dates are promised until a phase is in progress.

## Phase 1: Foundation (current)

- [x] Public site with live Robinhood Chain readings
- [x] Brand palette and mark sampled from supplied assets
- [x] Release gates for layout, tap targets, copy and URL safety
- [ ] Publish the contract-address slot and social accounts when they exist

## Phase 2: Markets

- [ ] Question format and resolution-rule template
- [ ] Yes/No market contracts with USDC collateral
- [ ] Price display for each market
- [ ] Independent review of the contracts

## Phase 3: Discovery

- [ ] Browse by topic: politics, sport, finance and more
- [ ] Odds history per question
- [ ] Watchlists and change alerts

## Phase 4: Depth

- [ ] Liquidity tooling for market creators
- [ ] Public data export of prices and resolutions
- [ ] Documentation for third-party front ends

## Not planned

Custody of user funds, leverage, and markets whose outcome cannot be stated as Yes or No.
