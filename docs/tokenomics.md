# Token notes

Status: **not stated**. As of this revision no Hunt token contract exists in public. The site shows the contract-address slot as pending, and this page will not invent figures to fill it.

## What will be published, and when

Nothing below is a commitment to issue a token. If one is issued, the following will be announced together, never piecemeal:

- Contract address on Robinhood Chain, shown in full and copyable from the top of the site
- Total supply and how it is divided
- Any vesting or lock terms, with the on-chain addresses that enforce them
- What holding the token does and does not do inside Hunt

## Guardrails for any future design

- **No hidden minting.** Supply changes must be visible on-chain and described here first.
- **Locks verifiable by anyone.** A lock claimed in prose must be checkable in a block explorer.
- **No yield promises.** Hunt will not describe a return it cannot show.
- **Separate from market money.** Market collateral is USDC. A token, if it exists, never stands in for the money backing a position.

## How to read the site meanwhile

The register section lists every external fact with its state. A fact marked absent has not been announced. Treat anyone claiming otherwise as unverified until the address appears on hunt.markets.
